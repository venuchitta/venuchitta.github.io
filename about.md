    <p>
      Hi, I'm <strong>Venu</strong> — a software engineer with ~11 years of experience building
      distributed systems at companies like <strong>Confluent</strong>, <strong>Google</strong>,
      <strong>Grab</strong>, <strong>Microsoft</strong>, and <strong>PayPal</strong>.
    </p>

    <p>
      I love coding and distributed system challenges. My work sits at the intersection of
      <em>correctness, scale, and performance</em> — building infrastructure that has to keep
      working when things go wrong, and keep being fast when things get busy.
    </p>

    <h2>What I work on now</h2>

    <p>
      At <strong>Confluent</strong>, I'm on the Traffic Infra team — the layer that enables
      north-south connectivity for Confluent products (Kafka, Flink, Schema Registry) and
      east-west connectivity across multi-cloud environments. I'm currently leading the build
      of a connecting mesh for seamless E/W data flow across clouds.
    </p>

    <h2>Where I've been</h2>

    <p>
      Before Confluent, I spent nearly four years at <strong>Google</strong> on the
      BigQuery Engine (Dremel) — working deep in query processing, optimization, planning,
      and execution. Before that, distributed data infra at <strong>Grab</strong>, large-scale
      caching platforms at <strong>Microsoft</strong> (powering Xbox Live and the Windows Store),
      and data infrastructure and marketing platforms at <strong>PayPal</strong>.
    </p>

    <h2>Background</h2>

    <p>
      I hold an M.S. in Computer Science from Utah State University and a B.Tech in
      Electrical &amp; Electronics Engineering from KL University in India. I speak English,
      Telugu (native), and a little Hindi.
    </p>
